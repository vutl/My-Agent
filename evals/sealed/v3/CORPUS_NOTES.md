# Sealed v3 corpus: notes for question authors

19 files in `raw/` (13 English, 6 Vietnamese). Formats: 7 native PDFs, 3 scanned PDFs, 1 slide-deck PDF, 4 DOCX, 3 Markdown, 1 TXT.
Topics do not overlap v1/v2: there are no macro, Fed/FOMC, ENSO, labour or administrative-unit documents, and no NLP/RAG papers.
`manifest.json` holds URLs, licenses, hashes, page counts and per-file verification. `build_scripts/` holds the full pipeline: `fetch.sh` → `build_corpus.sh` → `make_manifest.py`.
All page numbers below are 1-based PDF page indices, not the page numbers printed on the page.

## Things to know

- **Repaired DOCX:** the two Công báo DOCX files (TT 83/2026, TT 12/2026) use backslashes in their zip entry names, which python-docx rejects. The fix renamed the entries to `/`. No part contents changed. Their XML digital-signature parts are kept but no longer validate. The original downloads are reproducible from `sources.json` URLs.
- **Converted DOCX/MD:** Wikipedia pages were converted from REST-API HTML at fixed revisions (see `source_url`). The conversion kept headings, paragraphs, lists, figure captions, infoboxes and content tables. It dropped references, navboxes, charts and "see also"/link sections. Math is written as `$TeX$`.
- **Scanned PDFs** were made by rasterizing the native originals with PyMuPDF (`scan_pdf.py`), so they contain exactly the original content. They have no text layer: PyMuPDF and pypdf both return 0 characters, and each page holds a single image.
- **Verification** used PyMuPDF and pypdf, because poppler (`pdfinfo`/`pdftotext`) is not installed on this machine. python-docx was used for DOCX, and UTF-8 decoding for MD/TXT.
- **Not in the corpus**, so good targets for unanswerable questions: NĐ 81/2021 and NĐ 97/2023 (only cited by NĐ 238), the August 2026 STEO, a 2025 Condition of Education report, GW170817 (it appears only as a row in the Wikipedia table), RT-1/RT-2 papers, and the 2026 MOET standards assessment results.
- No document is longer than 60 pages. The largest file is the scanned STEO at 15.4 MB, and every file is under 40 MB.
