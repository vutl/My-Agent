# Sealed v5 corpus: notes for question authors and judges

20 files in `raw/` (13 English, 7 Vietnamese). Formats: 7 native PDFs, 3 scanned PDFs, 1 slide-deck PDF, 4 DOCX, 3 Markdown, 2 TXT.
Topics do not overlap v1-v4: no macro/central-bank, ENSO/climate, labour, administrative-unit, NLP/RAG, energy-outlook, education-statistics, higher-education, gravitational-wave, robot-policy, dengue, PEP, WASDE/agriculture-supply, bee, NTSB aviation, malaria, wheat, YOLO/MAE/ResNet, plant-protection/food-safety/vaccination/hospital-nutrition or MIT ML4H material. The library covers US road-safety statistics (two NHTSA editions) and an FHWA Safe System deck; three CC-BY papers (speech recognition, materials graph learning, epidemic modelling) with two project READMEs; a CLI tool manual (ripgrep); two historical farm-sewage bulletins; Hạ Long Bay (EN and VI Wikipedia) and EN water/sanitation in Vietnam; and Vietnamese legal texts on drainage, road order, culture statistics, tourism and statistics organisation.
`manifest.json` holds URLs, licenses, hashes, page counts and per-file verification (`all_verified: true`). `build_scripts/` holds the pipeline: `fetch_all.sh` -> `build_corpus.sh` -> `make_manifest.py` (`sources.json` is the metadata input). Run the Python parts with an interpreter that has PyMuPDF, python-docx, pypdf and bs4 (the repo's `backend/.venv`). The READMEs and the Government portal files can change upstream: `raw/` and the sha256 values in the manifest are authoritative.
All page numbers below are 1-based PDF page indices (equal to the printed page numbers for the three papers and the two NHTSA documents).

## Things to know

- **Scanned PDFs**: NHTSA 2022 (200 dpi colour) and Annex I (200 dpi grey) were rasterized by us from the native originals and contain exactly the original content; the NQ 263 programme was published by the Government as an image-only PDF (colour, red seal over the title). All three have no text layer (PyMuPDF and pypdf return 0 characters; one image per page).
- **Legacy encoding**: no PDF with a TCVN3/VNI (legacy font) text layer could be found among the current Government portal files; all Vietnamese native PDFs extract as normal Unicode. The Vietnamese scanned files are the closest equivalent (text only visible after rendering).
- **Slide deck**: the FHWA source (highways.dot.gov) blocks scripted downloads, so the same FHWA deck as hosted by the New Mexico DOT was used.
- **Drafts**: the circular, Annex I and the comparison docx are Government consultation drafts (number/date blank, "ngày 30/9/2026" on the comparison). The VBHN files show "Số: /VBHN-..." blank.
- **Converted DOCX** (Wikipedia) come from REST HTML at fixed revisions (see URLs); navboxes, references and "see also" sections were dropped; figures appear as captions only.
- **TXT**: Project Gutenberg boilerplate is part of both files.
- **Verification** used PyMuPDF and pypdf, python-docx and UTF-8 decoding; `manifest.json` has `all_verified: true`. Largest file: the NQ 263 scan (17.0 MB); longest document: VBHN 55 (74 pp).
- **Not in the corpus** (good targets for unanswerable questions): the WeNet 2.0 paper (cited by the WeNet README), Nghị định 88/2007/NĐ-CP, Nghị định 98/2019/NĐ-CP, 08/2022 and 85/2024 texts, the 1-page text of Nghị quyết 263/NQ-CP itself, Nghị quyết 26-NQ/TW, any 2024 NHTSA count, "Traffic Safety Facts 2023" annual compilation, the MatGL QET paper, any ripgrep release number.
